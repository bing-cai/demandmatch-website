## No wrong price. Not to your best customer.

- **AI writes words, not numbers.** Prices, quantities, warehouses and weeks come from code and your rules. The AI only writes the text around them.
- **Approve is locked while anything is off.** If the draft holds a number that is not in the offer, or a promise the offer does not make, it cannot be approved. The reason is shown next to it.
- **Margin below the band? Your team lead decides.** Below the band, approval goes to the team lead; below the floor, to the product manager. You see at once who needs to approve.

## Questions from sales.

### Does DemandMatch send emails by itself?

No. It prepares offers and replies. Once you approve, the draft sits in your Outlook and you send it yourself.

### Does DemandMatch replace my job?

No. It takes the searching, calculating and typing off your hands. The customers, the negotiation and the decision stay with you.

### What if it misreads a request?

When DemandMatch is unsure, it does not guess. The request goes to “Needs a look” with the reason in words. And every number in the draft is checked against the offer before you can approve it.

### Do I have to learn a new system?

Hardly. You work from one list for today and send from your own Outlook. SAP and the CRM stay as they are. DemandMatch only reads from them.

### Do our pricing rules apply?

Yes. Prices come from your rules: price band, scale, framework contract, plus freight to the delivery address. If the margin falls below, the team lead or the product manager decides.
